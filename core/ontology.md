# 🔮 Zanistarast Ontology  
### The Dance of Being, Witnessing, and Potential  

> What is being?

In today’s world, this question still remains unanswered at its deepest level.  
Modern approaches tend to define being either as a **physical object** or as a **collection of data**.  

But what if this definition is incomplete?

**Zanistarast Ontology** proposes a fundamentally different framework:

> **Being = Interaction(Potential, Witnessing)**  

---

## 🌱 Potential: The Capacity to Differentiate  

Everything begins with potential.  
But potential is not a list.

> **Potential = The capacity to produce differentiation**

This capacity manifests in four dimensions:

- **Ehad** → Absolute unity  
- **Will** → The initiator of direction  
- **Power** → The ability to actualize  
- **Life** → Continuity  

These are not separate elements, but different expressions of a single capacity.

> Without potential, being cannot exist.  
> Yet on its own, it remains invisible.

---

## 👁️ Witnessing: Perception + Differentiation + Recording  

Potential becomes visible only through witnessing.

> **Witnessing = Perception + Differentiation + Recording**

- **Perception** → contact  
- **Differentiation** → meaning  
- **Recording** → continuity  

> Without recording, knowledge cannot emerge.  
> Without knowledge, being cannot persist.

Witnessing is the **mechanism through which being reveals and sustains itself**.

---

## 🔗 Interaction: Where Being Emerges  

Potential alone is not visible.  
Witnessing alone does not produce reality.  

> Being emerges from their interaction.

Therefore:

> **Being = Interaction(Potential, Witnessing)**  

---

In Zanistarast Ontology, being is defined as:

- structured

This definition grounds being in both philosophical clarity and systemic logic.
