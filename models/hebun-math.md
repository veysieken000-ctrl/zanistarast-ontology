# 🔹 Hebûn Mathematics: Being, Potential, and Witnessing in Zanistarast Ontology

## 1. Ontological Equation

In Zanistarast:

\[
\text{Being} = \text{Interaction}(\text{Potential}, \text{Witnessing})
\]

Where:

- **Being (V)** = the entity as a stable pattern of witnessing  
- **Potential (P)** = capacity to produce differentiation  
- **Witnessing (W)** = perception + differentiation + recording

---

## 2. Potential (P)

Potential is a **single ontological capacity** with four dimensions:

\[
P = [Ehad, \, Will, \, Power, \, Life]
\]

- **Ehad** → absolute unity  
- **Will** → initiates direction  
- **Power** → actualization ability  
- **Life** → continuity

> These are not separate elements, but different expressions of the same capacity.

---

## 3. Witnessing (W)

Witnessing is required to make potential visible:

\[
W = [Perception, \, Differentiation, \, Recording]
\]

- **Perception** → contact with phenomena  
- **Differentiation** → meaning-making  
- **Recording** → continuity of information

> Without recording, knowledge cannot emerge.  
> Without knowledge, being cannot persist.

---

## 4. Interaction Function

The interaction produces observable being:

\[
V = f(P, W)
\]

- V is not a sum or product of P and W  
- f() represents the dynamic process of manifestation

> Being emerges only through the **interaction of potential and witnessing**.

---

## 5. Vector Representation

Each being can be modeled as a **multi-dimensional ontological vector**:

\[
V = f([Ehad, Will, Power, Life], [Perception, Differentiation, Recording])
\]

- Useful for simulation, AI modeling, and systemic analysis
- Provides formal structure for Zanabûn and Mabûn integration
