# 🔹 Knowledge (K) in Zanistarast Ontology: Mathematical Formulation

## 1. Definition

Knowledge is the result of **coherent witnessing over time**:

\[
K = \sum_{i=1}^{n} W_i \quad \text{s.t. coherence(W_i)}
\]

Where:

- \(W_i\) = individual witnessing instance  
- \(coherence(W_i)\) = logical consistency condition  
- \(n\) = number of witnesses or agents

> Single consciousness produces perception, multiple produce knowledge.

---

## 2. Knowledge Properties

1. **Timelessness**  
   - Knowledge transcends individual observation times  
   - Ensures continuity across generations

2. **Coherence**  
   - Incoherent or inconsistent witnessing is considered **false knowledge**:

\[
False\_Knowledge = \{ W_i \, | \, \neg coherence(W_i) \}
\]

3. **Persistence**  
   - Stable knowledge patterns contribute to **Being continuity**

---

## 3. Vector and Network Representation

- Each \(W_i\) is a vector:

\[
W_i = [Perception, Differentiation, Recording]
\]

- Collective knowledge can be modeled as a **network of witnessing vectors**:

\[
K = G(W_1, W_2, ..., W_n)
\]

- Enables simulation of **social, epistemic, and ontological structures**

---

## 4. Integration with Hebûn

- Knowledge interacts with Being and Potential:

\[
V = f(P, W) \quad , \quad K = \sum W_i
\]

- Forms the foundation for **Zanabûn layer** in the system  
- Supports **Mabûn (economic circulation) and Rasterast (governance)** simulations
