# 🤖 Mabun AI Layer  

## Purpose  

AI acts as:

> Circulation analyzer and optimizer

---

## وظائف (Functions)  

- detect bottlenecks  
- predict shortages  
- optimize distribution  
- monitor ethical imbalance  

---

## Data Inputs  

- production rates  
- consumption patterns  
- geographic distribution  
- temporal variation  

---

## Output  

- dynamic resource allocation  
- risk alerts  
- system adjustments  

---

## Principle  

AI does not control the system.

> It assists human ethical decision-making.
