# 🔹 Mabûn: Economic Circulation and Flow in Zanistarast Ontology

## 1. Definition

Mabûn represents **circulation and resource distribution**:

\[
Flow(R, t, x) = \text{Resource circulation over time and space}
\]

Where:

- \(R\) = resource quantity  
- \(t\) = time  
- \(x\) = spatial location  

> Circulation ensures dynamic balance, ethical distribution, and sustainability.

---

## 2. Principles

1. **Flow over Accumulation**  
   - Resources should circulate according to societal and ecological needs  
   - Prevents hoarding or exploitative accumulation

2. **Equitable Distribution**  
   - Each being receives according to fıtrat and contribution potential  
   - Full balance is impossible; circulation approximates justice

3. **Temporal-Spatial Allocation**  
   - Resources are distributed based on production frequency, location, and need  
   - Inspired by ecological flows and natural systems

4. **Ethical and Moral Oversight**  
   - Human and Rasterast evaluation ensures ethical allocation  
   - AI assists by simulating flows and detecting bottlenecks

---

## 3. Vector and Network Model

- Resource distribution can be represented as **multi-dimensional flow vectors**:

\[
R_{i}(t, x) = f(P_i, W_i, EthicalConstraints)
\]

- Enables simulation of:  
  - Individual consumption  
  - Societal circulation  
  - Environmental sustainability

- Supports **integration with Hebûn (Being) and Zanabûn (Knowledge)**

---

## 4. Simulation-ready Properties

- **Dynamic**: reacts to changes in resource production and societal needs  
- **Ethically bounded**: AI ensures flows remain within moral constraints  
- **Scalable**: can model local, regional, or global economies

---

## 5. Key Concept

> Mabûn is not perfect balance; it is **dynamic circulation**, reflecting ethical, ontological, and ecological realities.
