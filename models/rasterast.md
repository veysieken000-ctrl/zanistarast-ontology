# 🔹 Rasterast: Governance, Ethics, and Systemic Stability in Zanistarast Ontology

## 1. Core Architecture

Rasterast operates as a **multi-layered governance system**:

- **Main Councils**
  - Hüküm (Governance)
  - Ahlak (Ethics)
  - Ekonomi (Economy)
- **Sub-Councils**
  - Localized according to societal fıtrat  
  - Decisions adapt to local ontological readings
- **Decision Flow**
  1. Councils propose measures
  2. Sub-councils evaluate according to fıtrat
  3. AI simulates outcomes for stability and coherence
  4. Final decision approved by ethical humans

---

## 2. Principles of Justice

1. **Justice for the Right**  
   - Law protects the right itself, not individuals’ or groups’ honor
2. **Ethical Oversight**  
   - All actions filtered through ethical evaluation
3. **Natural Reset**  
   - Societies failing ethical/moral standards trigger automatic corrective mechanisms
4. **Human Centrality**  
   - Humans are core decision-makers; AI assists but does not override ethical judgment

---

## 3. AI Integration

Artificial Intelligence is the **analytical assistant**:

- **Simulation**
  - Predicts outcomes of council decisions
  - Detects ethical or circulation conflicts
- **Optimization**
  - Suggests flows to maintain systemic stability
- **Monitoring**
  - Tracks ethical alignment, resource flow, and societal health
- **Reporting**
  - Provides options without replacing human judgment

> AI maximizes efficiency and coherence while preserving human ethical responsibility.

---

## 4. Decision Workflow Example

1. Hüküm Council proposes a governance regulation  
2. Local sub-councils read and evaluate according to fıtrat  
3. AI simulates impacts on:
   - Economy (Mabûn)
   - Knowledge (Zanabûn)
   - Ontology (Hebûn)  
4. Recommendations returned to councils  
5. Ethical and ontological evaluation applied  
6. Decision finalized and implemented

---

## 5. Integration with Other Layers

- **Hebûn**: Rasterast ensures the stability of Being  
- **Zanabûn**: Knowledge is ethically validated  
- **Mabûn**: Circulation flows remain coherent and sustainable  

> Rasterast = Councils + Sub-Councils + AI as ethical assistant
