# The Core Law of Zanistarast: A Unified Information-Based Architecture of Physical, Biological, and Cognitive Systems

## Abstract

This paper introduces the foundational principle of the Zanistarast framework: 

> All systems operate through structured information-processing mechanisms that govern their behavior across scales.

Building on concepts from complex systems theory, information theory, thermodynamics, and systems biology, we propose a universal architecture:

> System = Core + Information-Control + Interaction

This framework unifies physical forces, biological regulation, and cognitive processes as manifestations of information-driven control.

We further formalize testable hypotheses and propose computational simulations to evaluate system stability, adaptability, and emergence.

---

## 1. Introduction

Modern science explains reality through multiple fragmented domains:

- Physics → forces and fields  
- Biology → genetic and regulatory systems  
- Cognitive science → perception and decision-making  

Despite their differences, all these domains exhibit:

- structured behavior  
- rule-based evolution  
- constrained dynamics  

This paper proposes a unifying claim:

> The universe is not merely governed by forces or matter, but by **information-structured control systems**.
