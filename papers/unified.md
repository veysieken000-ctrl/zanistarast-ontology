# Zanistarast Unified Framework: A Non-Random, Testable Model of Being, Knowledge, and Universal Order

## Abstract

This paper introduces the **Zanistarast Unified Framework**, a comprehensive model that integrates ontology, epistemology, economics, and governance into a single testable system.

The framework is based on a central claim:

> Existence is not random. It is structured, rule-governed, and systematically observable.

By defining being as the interaction of potential and witnessing, and by extending this structure across physical, biological, and cognitive systems, Zanistarast proposes a universal architecture of reality.

This study reformulates metaphysical intuitions into **computationally testable hypotheses**, enabling simulation, falsifiability, and scientific engagement.

---

Humanity has long attempted to understand existence through:

- science

- religion

> **to know (bilmek)**

Zanistarast does not treat these domains as separate. Instead, it proposes:

> Knowledge is unified, and reality can be studied through a single coherent framework.

This paper argues that:

- the universe is not random  
- systems are structured  
- human beings are not independent from this system
